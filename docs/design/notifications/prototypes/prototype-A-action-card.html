XTNL alert email redesign, design prototype (not wired into the engine). Made-up sample data.

Prototype A: Action card

Opens with one big coloured box: DO THIS NOW or NO ACTION NEEDED. Then three short labelled sections (what happened, why it matters, what happens next), a small facts table and a grey technical box for the operator.

A: Action card · B: Plain note · C: Status ticket · README.md compares them.

The same six alerts appear in every prototype. Each sample shows the inbox row, the phone push where one is sent, then the email itself (the part inside the grey frame is what the email sends).
