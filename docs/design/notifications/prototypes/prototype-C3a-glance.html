XTNL alert email redesign, design prototype (not wired into the engine). Made-up sample data. Developed from C2.

Prototype C3a: Glance

Everything you need fits on the first phone screen. The headline sits inside the coloured band, the first step is one big box, and the rest is one-line why, next and facts. The full detail stays in the operator line.

C3a: Glance · C3b: Timeline · C3c: Status board · README.md compares them.
