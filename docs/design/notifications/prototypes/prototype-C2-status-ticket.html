XTNL alert email redesign, design prototype (not wired into the engine). Made-up sample data.

Prototype C2: Status ticket, second iteration

The first word of every subject says who has to act. The coloured band repeats it with the part of the system that sent the alert and who it is for. Then come numbered steps, each saying where it is done, and a strip showing where the alert is in its life. 13 samples cover all six segments.

First version: prototype C · Rules and the full alert map: prototype-C2-spec.md

How to read the first word
ACTYou do something, at StoneX.
FIXThe operator does something, on the server PC or the broker setup.
FYINobody needs to do anything. For your records.
DONESomething that was open has closed.
The colour is the severity, separately from the word:
Urgent Important Heads-up Info Resolved
