XTNL alert email redesign, design prototype (not wired into the engine). Made-up sample data. Developed from C2.

Prototype C3c: Status board

The C2 ticket, then a board of the whole account at the moment of sending: halt level, open alerts by who acts, StoneX data and the server. Any email answers 'is everything else OK?'. Counts and states only.

C3a: Glance · C3b: Timeline · C3c: Status board · README.md compares them.
