# Broker Adapter Isolation Rules (Plugin Contract)

1. **Pure Translation Layer:** Concrete adapters (`StoneXAdapter`, `PepperstoneAdapter`, `MockBrokerAdapter`) implement `BrokerAdapter` and return canonical `OrderDTO`, `PositionDTO`, `PriceDTO`, `AccountDTO`, `CashFlowDTO` or `InstrumentSpec` objects (see `dto-contracts.md`).
   - Every broker quantity is converted to canonical units with `InstrumentSpec.to_notional()`. The specs come from the adapter's private, read-only map `self._instruments: dict[str, InstrumentSpec]`. `fetch_instruments()` fills it during SNAPSHOTTING, before any stream starts. Adapters never read instruments from Redis or PostgreSQL. A symbol missing from the map triggers `fetch_instruments()` for that adapter (rate-limited). If it is still unknown, the order or position is emitted with `instrument_unknown = true` and its raw broker quantity, so it is committed and Oversee returns `risk_unknown` → `SOFT_HALT`; it is not parked in the DLQ.
   - Adapters never compute risk.
   - Adapters pass through the broker's own record timestamp and revision when the broker supplies them, and set `source`. They never invent a timestamp and never assign `seq_id`; the orchestrator labels the version and stamps `seq_id`.
2. **Broker Mapping Rules:**
   - **StoneX:**
     - `ORDERS` items map to `OrderDTO`, with `client_order_id` = `broker_order_id` = the broker `OrderId` (accepted deviation: StoneX has no client-assigned id). The first item for a trade order that reports filled units > 0 **also** emits `PositionDTO(OPEN)` (`position_id = OrderId`, entry price read from REST `GET order/{id}/openposition`, else the `TradeOrder` of `GET order/{id}`, or `openpositions`). The read runs in the background while that item is held, like the parent read (never inline on the stream); a REST entry is never overwritten by a later item's `OpenPrice`. Any failed body read (403 / 80001, another broker error, a timeout) moves on to the next source; only an expired session stops the read. Accepted deviation (K8): when no source answers (or too many orders are held) the item maps with the stream's `OpenPrice` / `ExecutionPrice` rather than wait. The entry is corrected only when a later REST `openpositions` read (the next SNAPSHOTTING / RESYNCING, which may be hours away while the stream has no gap) teaches the mapper the REST entry via `learn_position`; the next ORDERS item for that order then carries it. The REST row itself may not overwrite the stream row (it is versioned by the position's `LastChangedDateTimeUTC`, which can be older than the item's `LastChangedTime`). StoneX `OpenPrice` on a filled trade is normally the fill price. Later items for that `OrderId` update both the `OrderDTO` and the `PositionDTO` until the order is terminal, then the `PositionDTO` only.
     - A position's version uses only a timestamp the stream item itself carries. If items carry none, StoneX positions are in the local clock domain.
     - `TRADEMARGIN` and `CLIENTACCOUNTMARGIN` feed `AccountDTO` (balance; `realised_pnl_today` stays `None`, see K9 below) and the live account fields only. `TRADEMARGIN` never builds a position.
       - Accepted deviation (K9, 2026-11-18): neither feed (nor REST `clientAccountMargin`) carries a realised-P&L field, so StoneX `realised_pnl_today` stays `None` (as on cTrader). It is never invented, never summed from the trade history (StoneX omits `RealisedPnl` on some closing trades), and no field is added to the proven CLIENTACCOUNTMARGIN list (an unknown field name fails the whole subscription). Realised P&L reaches the ledger as `TRADE_PNL` cash flows; no rule reads `realised_pnl_today`.
     - Closing trades (orders that close or offset a referenced position) are reduce-only. Their fills, like those of filled if-done children, **reduce or close** the linked `PositionDTO` and never emit a new one.
     - If-done stop / limit children stay **separate** reduce-only `OrderDTO`s with `linked_position_id` = the parent `OrderId` (which may still be a pending entry). Never fold a child stop into the parent's `stop_loss`.
     - **Stream parent of an if-done child** (fix 2026-11-21, live finding 2026-11-20: a sell-stop entry's stop and take-profit children were committed unlinked, so the entry and both children paged `missing_stop_loss`). In order:
       - the child's own `activestoplimitorder` `ParentOrderId`;
       - the IfDone / StopOrder / LimitOrder children nested in the row of any order the stream reads by id (a pending entry's child has no active row of its own);
       - a snapshot;
       - only then the bracket rule: a Pending stop / limit whose first `LastChangedTime` equals the first `LastChangedTime` of exactly one candidate on the same trading account and market, on the opposite side, and not itself a child, a bracket or a closing trade. An order is a candidate from the arrival of its first frame, even while that frame is held for its own read. Several candidates (an OCO straddle, two entries in one second) or a candidate whose side is still unknown are never guessed.
       - Only the bracket's own first `LastChangedTime` is matched, never a later item's time, because an order first seen after the bracket cannot be its parent.
       - A held child is mapped only after its known parent's held frames, or, while its parent is unknown, after every candidate of its key whose read is still running. A finished read is acted on only behind the broker frames already queued when it finished. Each child is therefore emitted once, after its parent, and is never re-emitted with a guessed link.
     - Accepted deviation (2026-11-21): a child nested in a **working entry's** IfDone (snapshot / RESYNCING) states no status or time of its own. It maps `PENDING` (as the stream states it) at the entry row's `LastChangedDateTimeUTC`, never an untimed `WORKING`. So a snapshot agrees with the stream, a later stream Pending is no illegal transition, and a child committed unlinked is healed by the next snapshot. A child that an older build's snapshot stored `WORKING` would read the new `PENDING` as an illegal transition. On 2026-11-21 none was stored (no non-terminal child of an unfilled entry), so no migration was needed.
     - `order_kind` (design PR1): order type Stop (2) → `STOP`, Limit (3) → `LIMIT`, a Trade (1) has none (`None`), from the row's own type field (stream, REST and the stop / limit history's `TypeId`); trade-history rows state no type (`None`). A row without a kind inherits the entity's known kind in the orchestrator (`dto-contracts.md` §2), so a stream item and a REST read of the same child never flip it.
     - Cash history (where available) maps to `CashFlowDTO`.
     - **Order history** (`fetch_history`, live probe 2026-11-14, read-only): `OrderReference` / `TradeReference` are empty on both `order/tradehistory` and `order/stoplimitorderhistory`, and `OrderApplicabilityId` is one constant, so no reference field links a row to another. An opening trade lists `OpeningOrderIds = [its own OrderId]` and no `RealisedPnl`: the own id is not a link (it is an opening trade, not a closing trade without P&L). A bracket is entry X (itself a stop / limit history row when it is a stop or limit entry), stop-loss X+1 and take-profit X+2 on the other side, created in the same second. A child's parent is taken, in this order, from `ParentOrderId`, from the trade history (a child that filled is a closing trade whose `OpeningOrderIds` name its entry), from the stream mapper's memo, and only then from the bracket rule (X = C-1 or C-2, and the whole bracket X, X+1, X+2 is in the stop / limit history: X not itself a child, one account, market and created second, X+1 and X+2 both on the side opposite X and neither an opening fill in the trade history, exactly one candidate); anything else (a one-leg bracket, an OCO straddle of two entries, a leg still working) stays unlinked and is never guessed (`src/adapters/stonex/history.py`). Each stop / limit history row yields a WORKING version at `CreatedDateTimeUtc` (its last `TriggerPrice` / `Price`: no amendment trail) before its terminal version; a filled one ends with the trade history's FILLED row at `ExecutedDateTimeUtc`.
   - **Pepperstone cTrader:**
     - Execution events map to `OrderDTO` and `PositionDTO`; positions carry native `stopLoss` / `takeProfit`.
     - An order or position whose status is present but not a known ProtoOA value is unmappable (counted in `unmapped_rows`, and on a stream a gap opens); an absent status is the proto default (ACCEPTED / OPEN), and CREATED / ERROR positions (never filled) are skipped.
     - `closingOrder` or an SL/TP order type sets `is_reduce_only`.
     - `order_kind`: LIMIT → `LIMIT`; STOP, STOP_LIMIT → `STOP`; STOP_LOSS_TAKE_PROFIT → `STOP` when its `stopPrice` is set (the stop-loss leg), else `LIMIT`; MARKET, MARKET_RANGE, unknown or missing → `None`. A position's own `stopLoss` stays the position's stop.
     - A **working** SL/TP (protection) order is not emitted as an `OrderDTO` (accepted deviation): the position's own `stopLoss` / `takeProfit` is the truth, so a stale protection order can never stand in for a stop the position no longer has. Terminal protection orders are still emitted.
     - `balanceVersion` is the account's `broker_seq`.
     - Quantities: ProtoOA `volume` (and the symbol's `lotSize` / `minVolume`) are in hundredths of a unit, so `units = volume / 100` (`unit_kind = CENTI_UNITS`; to be confirmed against the API version, with a mock fixture of a cTrader-encoded position).
     - `DepositWithdraw` maps to `CashFlowDTO`.
     - Broker equity is not available, so `equity` is `None`.
   - Per instrument, declare whether swap and commission are already booked to the balance (`swap_booked_to_balance`), so equity is not double-counted.
3. **Forbidden Imports:** Never import `src.db`, `src.cache`, `src.pubsub`, `src.integrity`, or `src.oversee` inside `src/adapters/`. Adapters must not publish to queues or write to PostgreSQL or Redis.
   - Nor `src.marketdata`, `src.riskmgr` or `src.armconfig`: the market-data layer and the ARM call adapters only through the read-only capabilities of §4 (`fetch_price_bars`, `fetch_active_stop_orders`) via `src/runtime/md_ports.py`, and adapters never know they exist.
4. **Read-Only Interface Lock:** Only implement:
   - `fetch_open_orders()`, `fetch_order_status(broker_order_id)`;
   - `fetch_positions()`, `fetch_position_status(position_id)`;
   - `fetch_account_snapshot()`, `fetch_cash_flows(since_ms)`, `fetch_instruments()`;
   - `fetch_history(since_ms, until_ms) -> BrokerHistory` (single-PC mode, `operations.md` §17; since plan S39b also the ARM's entry-time lookup, `IngestionOrchestrator.history_execution_time`, read-only, at reconcile priority): every order whose broker state changed in the window, terminal or not, and the positions opened or closed in it (CLOSED included), each with its own broker version, plus `covered_from_ms` (later than `since_ms` when the broker keeps less) and `complete` / `notes` (no amounts). Read-only, at reconcile priority on the REST budget. A position is never closed for being absent. The base default answers "history not supported" (`complete=False`). `orders` may hold several versions of one order, oldest first per entity (`BrokerHistory` contract). StoneX reads `order/tradehistory` (paged; incomplete while `STONE_X_HISTORY_PAGING_CONFIRMED` is off, because the time its `from` filter uses is unconfirmed) and, unless `STONE_X_STOP_LIMIT_HISTORY=0`, `order/stoplimitorderhistory` (confirmed live 2026-11-14; without it stop / limit orders finished while off cannot be listed, so the answer is incomplete), each stop / limit order with its placement and its links (§2 StoneX, order history). cTrader reads the order list and deal list (by position for closed positions; rejected deals move no position), capped at 13 weeks per request window;
   - `fetch_active_stop_orders() -> ActiveStopOrders` (design PR9, optional; off unless `XTNL_ARM_STOP_READS=1`): ONE list read per adapter, never one call per handle. StoneX: `GET order/activestoplimitorders` per trading account (inside `rest_get_only`); cTrader: the open positions from one reconcile request (their `stopLoss` is the handle). The base default answers `supported=False`. The orchestrator's `reread_stop_orders()` applies the rows like any REST read (origin `stop_read`, REST class BARS) and never closes an entity absent from the list;
   - `stream_ticks(symbols)`, `ensure_ticks(symbols)` (adds symbols to the live tick subscription; maps to cTrader subscribe spots or a StoneX add-subscription, both on the read-only allow-list), `stream_order_updates()`, `stream_positions()`, `stream_account()`;
   - `fetch_price_bars(symbol, side, timeframe_s, *, count=None, from_ms=None, to_ms=None) -> PriceBarsResponse` (optional, design PR3): the broker's own bars of one series in the count form (`count` complete bars, plus the forming one, which never counts towards `count`) or the range form, oldest first, the forming bar flagged `partial`. Base default: "not supported" (`supported=False`). Errors raise `BrokerError` (`PriceBarsDecodeError` for an answer that cannot be decoded). Never persisted, never logged with prices. Pepperstone keeps the default (not bar-capable in v1). StoneX implements it since S39 (PR3 probe, live_readonly, 2026-11-17): `GET market/{id}/barhistory` (count form) and `GET market/{id}/barhistorybetween` (range form), GET only (`rest_get_only()`), through the adapter's `ReadOnlyTransport` and REST budget at the caller's priority (`md-bars` reads at `BARS`); `BarDate` is the bucket start; bars carry no side tag (`BrokerBar.side` is `None`, the side is the request's). The PR3 probe (`python -m src.adapters.stonex.probe_bars`) reads through it; its `--check-stops` option compares the open positions' stop children with `fetch_active_stop_orders()` by order id only.

   Do not define order mutation methods. `ReadOnlyTransport` blocks per protocol:
   - REST (StoneX): block order-changing endpoints by **path**, not HTTP verb (StoneX `activeorders` is a read-only POST).
   - cTrader ProtoOA: an **allow-list of payloadTypes**, the one canonical list (the diagram's guard and ReadOnlyTransport refer to it): application auth, account auth, accounts by access token (scope check), refresh token, version, reconcile, order details / order list, deal list (and by position id), cash-flow history, trader, symbols list / by id, asset list, symbol schedule / holidays, subscribe / unsubscribe spots, heartbeat. To be confirmed against the API version; a contract test runs every `fetch_*()` through `ReadOnlyTransport`. Every other message is refused before it reaches the socket. Under `live_readonly`, start-up checks that the granted OAuth scope is `accounts` and refuses otherwise.
5. **Connection, Liveness & Reconciliation (SM4):**
   - Adapters run only on the engine leader. Every broker connect, LogOn and token refresh first checks the lease deadline synchronously (`operations.md` §2). Broker sockets use `SO_LINGER(on, 0)` and `TCP_USER_TIMEOUT ≤ 2 s`, so a fence resets them instead of flushing.
   - **Environment:** `BROKER_ENV` must be `sandbox` or `live_readonly`; anything else is refused at start-up. `live_readonly` lets the 24/7 server watch the real accounts, using read-only credentials or OAuth scope where the broker offers one (cTrader `accounts` scope). Order endpoints stay blocked by path and by interface either way.
   - **cTrader Open API** (Spotware ProtoOA over TLS, demo and live hosts, to be confirmed) is the Pepperstone integration. The older REST / WebSocket endpoints in the skill's `broker-endpoints.md` predate this design.
   - **AUTH_FAILED:** credential errors (bad password, PasswordChangeRequired, `invalid_grant`, TokenInvalidated after a fresh token read) are not network errors. At most 3 attempts, then stop and page (critical), with no backoff loop. The cTrader token pair is state: refreshed only by the leader, saved before use (`operations.md` §13). `AUTH_FAILED` is persisted per (adapter, credential generation: Secrets Manager version id or `token_generation`) in `adapter_auth_state`. It is a cluster-wide fact, never a node-health input and never a reason to abdicate. A new leader does not retry that adapter until the credential generation changes or an operator re-arms it.
   - **Market sessions (SM10):** at the open, STREAMING adapters run a REST-only RESYNCING with jitter (open + 30–60 s); the others go through SNAPSHOTTING. Price staleness is muted only while the symbol is closed or in its roll break.
   - Heartbeats per adapter (cTrader `ProtoHeartbeatEvent` every 10 s; StoneX Lightstreamer keepalive; no inbound traffic for 30 s drops the socket; values to confirm) and exponential backoff with jitter (`base * (2 ** attempt) + uniform(0, 0.5)`).
   - **AUTH_FAILED exit:** the only way out is AUTHENTICATING, when the credential generation in `adapter_auth_state` changes or an operator re-arms the adapter (`operations.md` §13, §16).
   - **Start-up, wake and reconnect catch-up (single-PC mode, `operations.md` §17):** before SM4 starts the leader holds the adapter's `stream_health` fence; after the first committed SNAPSHOTTING (or the RESYNCING forced after a sleep, or the pass after a mid-run interruption) the catch-up reads `fetch_history` from the last recorded coverage minus the overlap, submits every row through the orchestrator's normal REST path, re-reads by id what is in neither the snapshot nor the history, records `catchup_runs`, pages, then releases the fence. The mock adapter scripts it with `change_while_off`, `list_in_history`, `set_history_incomplete` and `set_history_supported`, and its streams serve again after a reconnect.
   - **SNAPSHOTTING** (every reconnect and re-authentication):
     - re-fetch instruments, open orders, positions, the account, and `fetch_cash_flows(since the current trading day's roll)` (idempotent on `transaction_id`, so a flow lost in a gap is never skipped because a later one committed first);
     - subscribe **each stream that is not `SUBSCRIBED`**, so a re-authenticated socket gets its subscriptions back and nothing is subscribed twice;
     - `stream_ticks(symbols)` includes the FX crosses needed for every `pnl_ccy` → account currency;
     - it also includes the market-data layer's **tick-only** symbols (`XTNL_TICK_SYMBOLS`, a separate orchestrator set, design PR5). They are never part of the held / required set: they never decide SM10's adapter OPEN, are never SM10 evidence, are not watched for stale prices or clean mids, and their ticks go only to the price hook, never to `market.price.dto` (so never to Redis price keys or `last_clean_marks`). Their `ensure_ticks()` is a call of its own whose failure is logged and retried; a per-market UNSUBSCRIBED / ERROR on one of them closes its interval and never opens the prices gap, and its SUBSCRIBED never marks the stream subscribed after a required market or the whole stream failed. When no required symbol is asked for, the stream starts with the empty required set, the tick-only symbols get their own `ensure_ticks()`, and the stream is trivially subscribed. A symbol removed from the set stays tick-only (retired) for the adapter's life, because the adapters subscribe every market they ever had again at each stream start;
     - while STREAMING, the orchestrator calls `ensure_ticks(symbols)` (an allowed read-only subscribe, rate-limited) when a committed order or position needs a symbol or cross that is not subscribed yet, so a pair first traded mid-session gets prices; no clean mid within 30 s pages;
     - any REST, socket or subscribe error → BACKOFF; lost authentication → AUTHENTICATING.
   - **On request** (an unexplained balance decrease seen by the RiskSweep, or an operator `refresh_cash_flows`, both relayed by the orchestrator, which owns the adapters), a STREAMING adapter runs `fetch_cash_flows(since the current trading day's roll)`, at most once a minute per account.
   - **Liveness** is per subscription (prices, orders + positions, account):
     - The orchestrator mirrors each subscription's health into PostgreSQL `stream_health.conn_lost_at` (set when it leaves `SUBSCRIBED` or a gap opens; cleared once it is `SUBSCRIBED` again and the reconcile committed). That durable row is what Oversee's fence reads; the `conn:` keys serve liveness alerts and the dashboard.
     - A subscription is `SUBSCRIBED` from the broker's ack until an `UNSUBSCRIBED` / `ERROR` frame **or the loss of its socket**. An `UNSUBSCRIBED` / `ERROR` frame while the socket is still up opens a **gap** for that stream: SM4 enters RESYNCING, which re-subscribes it first with backoff; after N failed re-subscribes → BACKOFF. An alert fires when any `conn:{adapter_id}:{stream}` key has been absent for more than 60 s while SM4 is STREAMING and the market is OPEN. Closing or losing a socket (entering BACKOFF) resets every subscription on it to not `SUBSCRIBED` and deletes its `conn:` keys, so a new socket starts with none.
     - Price subscriptions are also tracked **per market** (design PR6): a `StreamEvent` naming a `market` opens or closes that market's interval and resets only that market's subscription-snapshot tracking; a whole-stream SUBSCRIBED opens every market the adapter reports in `subscribed_price_markets` (else every requested market); every open / close timestamp is the orchestrator's clock; a socket / session loss, the end or restart of the prices pump and a whole-stream UNSUBSCRIBED / ERROR close them all. A downtime catch-up's fence hold never closes one. cTrader reports a symbol added by `ensure_ticks()` with its own SUBSCRIBED. Dropped price frames and lost updates are counted per market (never a gap).
     - On cTrader, the orders, positions and account subscriptions count as `SUBSCRIBED` from `ProtoOAAccountAuthRes` until `AccountDisconnect`, `TokenInvalidated` or `ClientDisconnect`; any of those sends SM4 to AUTHENTICATING.
     - The orchestrator refreshes `conn:{adapter_id}:{stream}` on the shared transport heartbeat only while that subscription is `SUBSCRIBED`. Positions share the orders key.
     - When the account is flat, a REST `fetch_account_snapshot()` every 60 s also proves the account path.
     - Price streams (design PR2, PR6): each `PriceDTO` carries `ts_source` (`BROKER` when the broker's tick time was read, `RECEIPT` when `timestamp_ms` fell back to receipt time) and `received_ms` (StoneX: stamped on the Lightstreamer thread when the frame arrives; cTrader: stamped by the socket reader). A per-market price subscription event names its canonical symbol in `StreamEvent.market` (StoneX PRICES subscriptions; the raw MarketId for a market without a known instrument, never `None`; `None` for whole-stream events such as a lost socket or the cTrader batch subscribe). Lost price updates are counted per market (`StoneXAdapter.dropped_price_frames`, with frames dropped for a full channel), never a gap. `XTNL_STONEX_PRICE_UNFILTERED=1` (off by default, turned on only after the PR3 probe) asks `setRequestedMaxFrequency("unfiltered")` on PRICES subscriptions only.
   - **Watermark reconciliation** (SNAPSHOTTING, RESYNCING, and SM3 RECONCILING):
     - record the adapter's stream watermark when the REST calls are sent;
     - re-read **by id** (`fetch_order_status`, `fetch_position_status`) every locally non-terminal order and `OPEN` position that is absent from the snapshot and whose stored version is ≤ the watermark. In the first SNAPSHOTTING of a process (no stream frame seen yet, so no watermark), **every** absent non-terminal entity is re-read by id;
     - **never close an entity just because it is absent.**
   - **RESYNCING** (after a gap: dropped frames, or a subscription lost while the socket is up; and at each market open): first re-subscribe only the streams that are not SUBSCRIBED (with backoff), then REST (open orders, positions, account, and cash flows since the current trading day's roll). The socket keeps draining; SUBSCRIBED streams and their consumer tasks are untouched.
     - A REST error, or frames dropped again after the watermark, retries with a new watermark. Retries are allowed only while the ring is below 50 % and before a deadline.
     - A socket error, the deadline passing, or N failed re-subscribes → BACKOFF (the socket is closed).
     - Leave to STREAMING only when every stream is SUBSCRIBED and no frame was dropped after the watermark.
   - Never stop draining a socket; support `backpressure_overflow` (drop price frames at the parser).
